Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Zero Data Collection

PrivacyPDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We use no tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or user behavior tracking tools. All visits are completely anonymous.

2. Local-Only Processing

All PDF files uploaded to this site are processed entirely within your browser. No file data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party servers. Once you close the browser tab, all data is permanently cleared from memory.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or storage services. Since we collect no data, there is nothing to share.

4. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Right to Access: No data is collected, so nothing to access.
  • Right to Deletion: No data is stored, so nothing to delete.
  • Right to Object: No data processing occurs.
  • Right to Portability: Not applicable — we hold no data.

We are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant by design — not by policy. Privacy is built into the architecture.

5. Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. We collect no data from any user regardless of age.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our social media channels.
