About PrivacyPDF

PrivacyPDF is a free, privacy-first PDF compression tool. Unlike traditional online tools that upload your files to external servers, PrivacyPDF runs entirely in your browser — locally, securely, and with zero data retention.

Why PrivacyPDF?

Most online PDF tools require uploading documents to the cloud, exposing sensitive information to potential leaks. PrivacyPDF eliminates this risk by processing all files locally on your device. Your contracts, invoices, medical records, and personal documents never leave your computer.

Our Commitment

  • 100% Local Processing — Files never leave your browser
  • Zero Data Collection — No logs, no tracking, no storage
  • Free to Use — Core features permanently free
  • Cross-Device — Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

Our Philosophy

We believe digital tools should be private by design. Privacy isn't an afterthought — it's the foundation of everything we build.
